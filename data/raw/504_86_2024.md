## At a glance

The Ecosuntek Group, founded in 2008 as a supplier of EPC services for the photovoltaic market, is active in the renewable energy sector and manages the entire energy supply chain, from production to distribution and sale to the private sector and business on national territory. Over the years the Company has specialized in the following activities:

• Power generation: the Company produces electricity from owned photovoltaic systems, with a total installed capacity of 17 MW (12 MW in Italy and 5 MW in Romania);

• Electricity sales and reselling: in 2015 the Company undertaken the activity of reseller of electricity (now carried out through the subsidiary Eco Rrade S.r.l.), managing a customer portfolio of around 200 MWp, and in 2024 finalized the acquisition of +Energia, active in electricity direct sales;

• Natural Gas Distribution: in 2021, Ecosuntek has inaugurated a specialized Desk, in order to integrate the own electricity marketing activities and gas with an energy asset management division.

## Activities and Services Offered

## 7

Power generation: The Company manages directly and through vehicle companies of the Group proprietary plants, for a total of 17 MW of installed capacity, of which 12 MW in Italy and 5 MW in Romania. The Power Generation activity is the one with the highest level of margins. The Group is planning the development and construction of new industrial-scale ground-mounted photovoltaic plants.

Sales and Reselling: This activity, managed by the subsidiary Eco Trade, consists in the purchase of energy on the producers' market (mainly from renewable sources) and the subsequent resale to market operators. Through the recent acquired subsidiary +Energia (46.775%), one of the primary energy companies operating in the free market, Ecosuntek is now selling electricity also to retail.

Natural Gas Distribution: This activity, managed by the subsidiary Eco Sviluppo, consists of marketing on the hubs and regulated platforms (for example PSV and PCE), of three commodities: natural gas (main) electricity, and CO 2. In 2021, the Group integrated the Sales activity with an energy asset management division.

## Direct sales

## 12

+Energia has been operating in the free market for the sale of electricity for around 15 years and is a company accredited by the Electricity, Gas and Water System Authority. Since its establishment in 2008, +Energia has proven itself as a dynamic and innovative firm, quickly becoming one of the primary energy companies operating in the free market.

Being close to the customer and therefore having a real presence in the territories, the company has opened various operational commercial offices: Rome, Milan, Bologna, Civitanova Marche, Avellino and Pescara. They collaborate with the offices of +Energia's business partners operating in the main Italian cities and the company's headquarters in Umbria.

## The Italian photovoltaic market

## 16

• As at December 31, 2022, 1,225,431 photovoltaic plants were installed in Italy, for a total power of 25,064 MW. Plants with power lower than or equal to 20 k W make up 93% of the total in terms of number and 26% in terms of power; the average size of the plants is just over 20 k W).

• During 2022, 210,555 photovoltaic systems were installed throughout the country - the vast majority of them smaller than 20 k W - for a total power of 2,490 MW 2; 20% of the installed power in 2022 is made up of plants larger than 1 MW.

• At the end of the year, 55% of the plants in operation in Italy were installed in the Northern regions, 17% in the Center and the remaining 28% in the South. The regions with the highest number of plants are Lombardy, Veneto, Emilia Romagna, Piedmont and Lazio.

## Photovoltaic energy production in Italy

## 17

• During 2022, around 210,000 photovoltaic systems were installed in Italy, for a total power just under 2,500 MW. At the end of the year, the total installed power in operation amounted to 25,064 MW, an increase of 10.9% compared to 2021. The production recorded in the year, on the other hand, amounted to 28,121 GWh; in this case, the increase compared to 2021 is +12.3%.

• In 2022, the region with the highest photovoltaic production is Puglia, with 4,190 GWh (14.9% of the 28,121 GWh produced overall at national level). Lombardy follows with 2,984 GWh and Emilia Romagna with 2,616 GWh, with a contribution equal to 10.6% and 9.3% respectively of the country's total production.

# ESG

## 23

• In July 2023 a path to formalize the initiatives and results obtained in 2022, Ecosuntek has published the first Sustainability Report: a tool aimed at measuring and monitoring, also with a future perspective, the Group's compliance with those that are considered priority issues in the life of the country and its citizens, first and foremost that of sustainability, which is already and will be increasingly central and characterizing for us.

• The Report was drawn up according to the "Global Reporting Initiative Sustainability Reporting Standards", updated to 2023.

• The Ecosuntek Group presents a high sustainability profile. In fact, the company offers its customers the possibility of self-producing renewable energy, allowing them to abandon energy from non-renewable sources, thus helping to mitigate the effects of climate change.

• The focus of the Group's sustainability policy is the valorization of human resources. In the last year the number of employees has remained stable with a retention rate in the reporting period of 100%. The company also provides a benefit in the form of health insurance (Metasalute) for employees, possibly hired on a part-time contract.
