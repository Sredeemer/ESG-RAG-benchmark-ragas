## YTD: year-to-date (since beginning of the year)

November saw increased volatility due to changing FED policy expectations and debates over AI capital expenditure returns. US, Japan, and emerging markets remained resilient, while Europe underperformed. The month stood out for the diverging performance of technology stocks and renewed focus on active diversification. Monthly performance In November the fund performed positively with 0.1%. Bond markets benefited from increased demand for duration as investors hedged against labor market risks. US Treasuries were upgraded, and short-tenor bonds gained favor, reflecting a cautious stance amid ongoing uncertainty. YTD performance YTD as of the end of November, the fund has delivered a positive performance of 2.0%. Fund exhibits a positive performance with cash, currency position, and currency hedging strategy being the main performance drivers. Allocation to Sustainable Bonds and Sustainable Finance Bonds posted the strongest returns. Performance contributors Within Fixed Income Corporate Bonds and Sustainable Bonds contributed most to the funds monthly positive performance with Multilateral Development Bank Bonds and (MDB) Sustainable Finance Bonds also posting positive returns. Performance detractors Our Cash, currency position, and currency hedging strategy as well as allocation to High Grade Bonds had a negative impact on the fund's return in the month of November.
